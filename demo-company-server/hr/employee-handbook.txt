Nexora Systems employee handbook — synthetic demo document.

Working hours are 9:30 to 18:30 Monday to Friday. Employees must use approved company devices, enable multi-factor authentication and report suspected security incidents to IT within one hour.

Leave: employees receive 18 annual paid leaves, 12 sick leaves and public holidays according to the applicable location calendar. Manager approval is required before planned leave. HR maintains employee records and compensation information; these records are not company-wide data.

Performance reviews occur twice a year. Promotion recommendations require manager evidence, calibration and HR review. Do not share another employee's compensation, performance rating or personal information.
